The guides say they were tested with **Crunchy PostgreSQL Operator 5.8.x** and use `postgres-operator.crunchydata.com/v1beta1` manifests. Crunchy's current release is **v6.0.3** (Aug 2026). Crunchy has also announced final planned releases of 5.6–6.0 around 31 Dec 2026, so most migrating customers will be on 5.8 or 6.0.

We ran all three methods against Crunchy **v6.0.3**. They work once the traps in the related issues are handled. v6 still serves `v1beta1` (the storage version), so the docs' manifests apply. The v6 examples and many GitOps repos use the new `v1` API, though.
