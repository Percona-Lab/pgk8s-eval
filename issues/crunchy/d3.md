Page: `migrate-from-crunchy-standby.md`

**1. The rollback section is inaccurate about post-cutover writes.** It says: *"Any writes that landed on `cluster1` after you promoted Percona are **not** on Crunchy."* With the shared pgBackRest repo this is not true. While Crunchy is in standby and points at the shared repo, it replays the WAL that the promoted Percona cluster archived there. In our test, after Crunchy was taken out of standby, it contained the row `'first write on percona'` that had been written to Percona after promotion. Readers who follow the current text may "copy data out manually" and duplicate it. Please describe what actually happens, and when it doesn't, e.g. if Crunchy was promoted before Percona's WAL reached the repo.

**2. Warn about two writers on one repo.** If rollback step 2 (put `cluster1` back into standby) is skipped or fails, both clusters archive into the same stanza on diverging timelines with no error. We reproduced this: Crunchy on timeline 7 and Percona on timeline 6, both `failed_count 0`. Ask readers to verify `pg_is_in_recovery() = t` on `cluster1` before un-standbying Crunchy. Product guard requested in percona-postgresql-operator.

**3. Say that Percona keeps writing into Crunchy's repo path after promotion.** Recommend switching `cluster1` to its own repo path once the post-migration backup policy starts.

**4. Certificates.** `customTLSSecret` / `customReplicationTLSSecret` reuse Crunchy-issued certificates (subject `hippo-primary.crunchy.svc...`, issuer `postgres-operator-ca`, valid 1 year). After cutover, `verify-full` clients connecting to `cluster1-primary` fail on the hostname, and nothing renews these certificates. Add a post-cutover step to switch to operator-managed or cert-manager certificates.

**5. Passwords.** Without copying `<crunchy>-pguser-<user>` Secrets first, the promoted cluster gets new passwords and applications fail to log in. Add the copy step before creating the standby.

**6. Small errors:**
- *Make a full backup...*: the numbered list starts at **2.** (step 1 is missing).
- The `kubectl wait job` command passes `-l` twice. kubectl keeps only the last selector, so it can match another cluster's manual backup job. Use one comma-separated selector.
- *Take a post-migration backup*: step 1 edits `deploy/backup.yaml`, but step 2 applies `deploy/post-migration-backup.yaml`.

**Measured with the guide (Crunchy v6.0.3, 332 MB):** standby streaming 55 s after creation, cutover write downtime about 13 s, no data loss.

Evidence (scripts, logs): https://github.com/Percona-Lab/pgk8s-eval (`crunchy/`, `results/mig*`, `FINDINGS.md`). Tested with Percona Operator 3.1.0 and Crunchy PGO v6.0.3 on k3s.
