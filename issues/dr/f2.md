## Feature request: detect and repair a diverged former primary in a standby cluster, and guard against two primaries across clusters

**Environment:** Percona Operator 3.1.0 (namespace-scoped installs in `pct-a` and `pct-b`), PG 18.6 (18.6.1-3), pgBackRest 2.59.1, k3s 1.35.9, S3-compatible object store. One instance per cluster; the two namespaces simulate two sites.

## Problem 1: diverged demoted primary is stuck, and status does not say so
In the repo-only switchover above (see the related feature request), the former primary diverged by its shutdown checkpoint. It stayed in a restore loop for minutes (`new timeline 2 forked off ... before current recovery point 0/70000A0`) with no replication. `kubectl get pg` gave no indication. With a streaming source, Patroni repaired the same situation automatically with pg_rewind. Without one, nothing happens.

Suggestions:
- A `StandbyDiverged` condition and Event when the standby cannot follow the source timeline.
- An opt-in automatic repair: pg_rewind against the new primary when a streaming host is configured, otherwise reinitialise from the repo (`patroni.removeDataDirectoryOnDivergedTimelines` covers in-cluster replicas but not this case).

## Problem 2: nothing prevents two primaries across clusters
Setting `standby.enabled: false` on a standby cluster is accepted even while the source cluster is still a primary writing to the same repo. Related: #1862, which covers archiving into one repo from two writers. CloudNativePG has the same gap: in our test a demotion was rejected by its webhook while the other side's promotion was accepted, and two primaries ran for 170 s.

Suggestion: before promoting a standby cluster, check the repo (and the streaming source, if configured) for a newer timeline or recent WAL from another writer. Refuse, or require an explicit override.

Evidence (scripts, logs, manifests): https://github.com/Percona-Lab/pgk8s-eval (see `dr/`, `results/dr_*`, `FINDINGS.md` → "Cross-site DR switchover").
