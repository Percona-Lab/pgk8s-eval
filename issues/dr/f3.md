## Feature request: delayed standby clusters

The limitations page says delayed replicas are not supported. CloudNativePG supports them (`recovery_min_apply_delay` on replica clusters), and DR reviews ask for them as protection against logical damage (`DROP TABLE`, bad migrations). Without one, recovery from such an error is a PITR from backup.

Proposal: `standby.applyDelay` (duration) on standby clusters. It would map to `recovery_min_apply_delay` on the standby leader and its replicas, and be reported in `status.standby` along with the current apply lag. It also needs a documented "fast-forward and promote" procedure: unset the delay, wait, promote.

Context: cross-site DR comparison with CloudNativePG 1.30.1. Evidence (scripts, logs, manifests): https://github.com/Percona-Lab/pgk8s-eval (see `dr/`, `results/dr_*`, `FINDINGS.md` → "Cross-site DR switchover").
