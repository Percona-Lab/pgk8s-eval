Crunchy compatibility and ideas to borrow

Percona's operator and Crunchy PGO share most of their code. The data on disk and the running cluster are fully compatible, verified by restoring, streaming from and reusing the volumes of a Crunchy v6.0.3 cluster. The CRD differs mostly in naming. The two products diverge in their images and operational tooling. Crunchy has also built several features since the fork that would help every Percona user, not only migrating ones.

One switch for compatibility

Three of the seven migration traps (dropped extensions, dormant cron jobs, and most of the smaller behaviour changes) come from Percona defaults that differ from Crunchy's. Rather than ask every migrating user to find them, bundle the Crunchy behaviour behind one field.

The same field gives a migration assistant (#1863) and in-place adoption (#1864) one thing to set. Users can switch it off once their applications are updated.
