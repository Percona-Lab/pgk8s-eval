Product and documentation recommendations

Win on reliability. Fix what hides it.

In a hands-on comparison, Percona's Patroni-based HA restored writes faster than CloudNativePG in 7 of 8 failure scenarios and kept failing over with the operator down. The same tests found two defects that turn a 30-second failover into minutes, status that reports ready while backups are broken, and documentation that is silent on failure behaviour. Follow-up tests on 14 Oct added a differentiator to protect: under a partition that left CloudNativePG 1.30.1 running two primaries for 129 s, Patroni never overlapped. They also showed what it still costs: with async replication, writes acknowledged in the 10–19 s before demotion are lost. These 36 recommendations close the gaps, protect the lead and remove the tooling gap against CloudNativePG. Cross-site DR items (P16–P21, D15) come from follow-up switchover and unplanned site-loss tests.
